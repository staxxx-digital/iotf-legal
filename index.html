Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

iOTF is a group travel planning application developed and operated under the Staxxx Digital brand. This Privacy Policy explains what information iOTF collects, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

For privacy or support questions, contact support.iotf@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use iOTF, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information such as your display name, profile photo, and trip membership.
  • Trip content such as destinations, itinerary items, trip ideas, votes, milestones, group messages, and shared expense information.
  • Private money information you enter for your own budgeting, including personal trip budgets and personal expenses. This information is designed to remain private to your account.
  • Media you choose to upload, including trip photos, videos, and profile images.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and maintain account security.
  • Information you choose to submit to ASK iOTF, including relevant authorized trip context used to answer your request.

How we use information

  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • provide trip planning, itinerary, group chat, voting, milestone, budget, expense-splitting, and media features;
  • personalize authorized trip context within the app;
  • provide ASK iOTF responses when you choose to use the AI feature;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability;
  • process account deletion requests and related cleanup; and
  • respond to support and privacy requests.

AI features

iOTF includes an optional AI feature called ASK iOTF. Before first use, the app presents an AI disclosure and asks for your consent. When used, ASK iOTF may receive bounded trip information that your account is authorized to access, including your own private budget information and recent trip chat context when relevant to your request.

ASK iOTF is not designed to disclose another user's private personal-budget data to you. iOTF does not send Photo Dump image or video file contents to the AI model as part of the current ASK iOTF feature.

AI responses may be inaccurate. Users should verify important travel, price, reservation, safety, and scheduling information before relying on it.

Service providers

iOTF relies on third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate the app. These may include Supabase for authentication, database, server-side functions, and private file storage; OpenAI for ASK iOTF AI responses; and Google Places / Google Maps services for place discovery and directions-related functionality.

These providers process information only as needed to provide the relevant service, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing and disclosure

We may disclose information to other members of a trip when it is part of a shared trip feature; to service providers that help operate iOTF; when required by law or legal process; when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, integrity, or security; or in connection with a business reorganization or transfer, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

Private budgeting and shared expenses

iOTF separates private personal budgeting from group expense-splitting features. Your personal trip budget and personal expenses are intended to be visible only to your account. Shared expenses and participant balances may be visible to members of the applicable trip.

Media

Trip media and profile images are stored using private storage controls. Access is limited according to app permissions and trip membership rules. Users should avoid uploading sensitive information that is not necessary for trip planning.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide iOTF, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, and support legitimate operational needs.

Some shared trip records may be preserved after a user deletes an account when deletion of the record would disrupt other users' shared trip history. In those cases, the deleted user's identity may be removed or replaced with a neutral label such as Deleted member where appropriate.

Account deletion

Users can initiate account deletion from within iOTF. Account deletion is designed to remove the deleting user's account, private budgeting information, owned profile image, owned Photo Dump media, messages, votes, and membership records, subject to shared-data preservation rules and technical safeguards.

If a user is the sole trip leader for a trip that still has other members, the user must select an eligible successor before deletion can complete.

Security

We use access controls, private storage, row-level database security, authenticated server functions, and other technical safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access. No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and notify us of suspected unauthorized access.

Children's privacy

iOTF is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the service.

U.S. users and state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, portability, or certain uses of personal information. To submit a privacy request, contact support.iotf@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

International use

If you use iOTF outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as iOTF evolves. The updated version will show a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app when appropriate.
